Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Who we are

The Recommender is a movie and TV recommendation service. We help you decide what to watch by asking a few quick questions about your mood and taste. We are committed to being clear and honest about the information we handle. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@therecommender.app.

Information we collect

We keep things minimal. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email address, or any identifying details to use the service.

Quiz answers. When you use the personalization quiz, we receive your selections — the type of content (movies or series), moods, hard no's, runtime preference, and any favorite titles you pick or type. These answers are used in real time to generate your recommendations. We do not store them or link them to you.

Search queries. When you search for a title, the words you type are sent to our server to find matches. As with any web request, this may appear in standard server access logs.

Technical data. Like most websites, our servers automatically log routine information such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request, and timestamps. We use this for security, troubleshooting, and understanding how the site is used at an aggregate level.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

• Generate movie and TV recommendations based on your quiz answers;

• Provide search and browsing features;

• Maintain, secure, and improve the service; and

• Respond to support requests.

Cookies & local storage

The Recommender does not set cookies. The Watchlist and Favorite buttons on our title pages save the titles you choose in your browser's local storage so the state persists between visits; this data stays on your device and is never sent to us. The Share button only copies the page link to your clipboard. You can clear this local data at any time through your browser settings. Third-party services we use (listed below) may set their own cookies or use local storage in line with their own policies.

Third-party services

To run the site, we rely on a few third-party services. Each one processes data under its own terms and privacy policy:

• Google Fonts — used to load the site's typography. See Google's privacy policy.

• Bootstrap and Bootstrap Icons — UI components and icons, loaded from the jsDelivr CDN. See jsDelivr's privacy policy.

• TMDB (The Movie Database) — the source of our catalog, posters, and artwork, served from image.tmdb.org. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. See TMDB's privacy policy.

• Hosting & database provider — the server that hosts this site and its database. Their handling of server logs and infrastructure data is governed by their own policies.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We only share data with the third parties described above to operate the service, and we may disclose information if required to do so by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the service and the information handled by it, including encrypted connections where supported by your hosting setup and restricted access to our database. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

Because we do not collect personal data through your use of the service, there is nothing for us to store or delete on your behalf. Server access logs are kept only for as long as needed for security and operations, in line with our hosting provider's practices, and are not used to identify individual visitors.

Children's privacy

The Recommender is a general audience service and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

Your choices & rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding the information we hold about you, including the right to access, correct, or delete it, and to ask questions about how it is used. Because we don't collect personal data from visitors, in most cases there is nothing additional to change or delete.

If you have any request or concern, email us at privacy@therecommender.app and we'll respond promptly.

International transfers

This service is available worldwide and may be hosted on infrastructure located in different countries. By using the service, you acknowledge that the third-party services we rely on may process data in countries outside your own, subject to their respective privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the service, the law, or our practices. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always show when it was last revised, and significant changes will be highlighted on this page.

Contact us

Questions, comments, or requests about this policy? Reach us at privacy@therecommender.app. Thank you for trusting The Recommender. Happy watching!
